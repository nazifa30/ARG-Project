Bolani

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Potato (boiled and mashed)
  • Green onions (chopped)
  • Fresh cilantro (chopped)
  • Oil (for frying)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Make dough with flour and water; let it rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Prepare the dough.
  3. Make the filling.
  4. Divide the dough into small balls.
  5. Roll out the dough.
  6. Add the filling to the center.
  7. Seal the bolani.
  8. Cook in a skillet until golden brown.
  9. Drain excess oil.
  10. Serve hot.

Manto

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Salt
  • Water (as needed)
  • Ground beef or lamb
  • Onion (finely chopped)
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Cumin powder
  • Pepper (to taste)
  • Yogurt
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh cilantro (for garnish)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the dough with salt and water; not too soft.
  2. Fry minced meat with onion, garlic, salt, and black pepper until cooked.
  3. Make the filling.
  4. Divide the dough and roll into thin circles.
  5. Add the filling to the center.
  6. Fold and seal the manto.
  7. Arrange in a steamer.
  8. Steam for about 30 minutes.
  9. Serve with yogurt and tomato sauce.

Qabli Palo

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Lamb or beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Cumin
  • Coriander
  • Black pepper
  • Carrots
  • Raisins
  • Almonds
  • Saffron (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice and soak for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown the meat with onions and garlic.
  3. Add spices and water; simmer until meat is tender.
  4. Cook the rice in salted water until partially done.
  5. Layer the rice over the meat in the pot.
  6. Add carrots, raisins, and almonds on top.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat until rice is fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot.

Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Baking soda
  • Salt
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Vanilla extract
  • Boiling water

Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix the dry ingredients.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla; mix well.
  4. Stir in boiling water until smooth.
  5. Pour batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for 30-35 minutes; check with a toothpick.
  7. Let cool before frosting.
  8. Serve and enjoy!

Apple Pie

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Apples
  • Cinnamon
  • Nutmeg
  • Lemon juice
  • Egg (for egg wash)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, salt, and sugar; cut in butter.
  3. Add water to form a dough; chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Peel and slice apples; toss with sugar and spices.
  5. Roll out dough and place in a pie pan.
  6. Add apple filling and cover with top crust.
  7. Cut slits for steam; brush with egg wash.
  8. Bake for 45-50 minutes until golden.
  9. Let cool before serving.

Raisin Cookie

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Brown sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • Baking soda
  • Salt
  • Cinnamon
  • Raisins

Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream together butter, sugar, and brown sugar.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla; mix well.
  4. In another bowl, combine flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
  5. Gradually add dry ingredients to the wet mixture.
  6. Fold in raisins.
  7. Drop spoonfuls onto a baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 10-12 minutes until golden.
  9. Let cool before serving.

Afghani Doogh

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Mint leaves
  • Cucumber
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Making Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together yogurt and water until smooth.
  2. Add salt to taste.
  3. Finely chop mint leaves and cucumber; mix into the yogurt.
  4. Chill in the refrigerator if desired.
  5. Serve over ice cubes if preferred.

Orange Juice

Ingredients

  • Fresh oranges
  • Sugar (optional)
  • Water (optional)
  • Ice (optional)

Making Instructions

  1. Wash the oranges thoroughly.
  2. Cut the oranges in half.
  3. Juice the oranges using a juicer or manual method.
  4. Taste and add sugar or water if desired.
  5. Serve chilled over ice if preferred.

Cappuccino

Ingredients

  • Espresso
  • Hot milk
  • Foamed milk
  • Cocoa powder or cinnamon (optional)

Making Instructions

  1. Brew a shot of espresso.
  2. Heat the milk until hot but not boiling.
  3. Froth the milk until it’s creamy and foamy.
  4. Pour the hot milk over the espresso.
  5. Add the foamed milk on top.
  6. Sprinkle cocoa powder or cinnamon if desired.
  7. Serve immediately.
